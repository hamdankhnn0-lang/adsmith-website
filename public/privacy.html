Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

This policy explains what we do with the information you give us when you use this website or contact us. Adsmith is a marketing agency based in Peshawar, Pakistan.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Through the contact form: your name, email address, company name if you give one, the service you are interested in, and whatever you write in the message field.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number, your name as it appears on your WhatsApp profile, and the contents of your messages.
  • If you email or call us: whatever you include in that contact.

We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website. We do not build a profile of you from your visit.

Why we use it

To reply to you, to prepare the audit or proposal you asked for, and to carry out work for you if you become a client. We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing emails to people who have not asked for them.

Who else sees it

Your enquiry passes through the services that run this website, for example our web host and the service that delivers form submissions to our inbox. They process it on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for anything else. If you contact us on WhatsApp, that conversation also sits with Meta under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within two years. Client records are kept for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as we need them for tax and accounting purposes. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email adsmithsolutions@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted connection. We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This website is aimed at businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
